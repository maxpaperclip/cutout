    Shortcuts

    V select · H pan · R rectangle · E ellipse · P polygon · S star · L line · B pen · N pencil · T text · D perspective.
    With the pen, click for corners and drag for curves; click the first point to close, or press Enter to finish. Double-click a shape (or press Enter) to edit its points.
    Ctrl+G groups the selection and Ctrl+Shift+G takes a group apart. Double-click a grouped object to pick just that object.
    With the pencil, end where you started to make a filled shape. Esc stops drawing.
    Shift while drawing keeps shapes square. Shift while turning snaps to 15°. Shift while tilting moves two corners together. Shift+H and Shift+V mirror. Arrow keys nudge. Delete removes.
